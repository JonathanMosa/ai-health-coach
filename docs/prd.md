# AI Health Coach: Product Requirements Document

**Project** | AI Health Coach 
**Version** | 1.0
**Last Updated** | 05/20/2026
**Author** | Jonathan Mosa
**Status** | Draft

---

## Problem Statement

This app brings consistency with health into the average person's life without them having to plan a single thing.

---

## Target Users

This app is designed for beginners in the gym, or people who are trying to optimize their performance toward specific fitness goals. The target user is someone looking for consistency but lacks the time or knowledge to plan on their own.

**Personas:**
- A **college student** who wants to make progress in the gym but cannot afford a personal trainer.
- A **busy worker** who lacks the time and knowledge to take their health journey seriously.

---

## Core Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Daily Check-In Prompts** | A form that asks the user questions about sleep, soreness, and energy level from the previous day. |
| **LLM-Generated Coaching Plans** | A weekly workout plan generated by an AI model based on the user's goals and check-in history. |
| **Progress Dashboard** | Displays a weekly progress bar and streak for completing workouts and daily check-ins. |
| **Email / SMS Reminders** | Reminds the user of their goals for the week and prompts them to complete the daily check-in if not done within 24 hours. |
| **Workout Logs** | Takes input of workouts completed on a day-to-day basis and saves them, tracking which muscle groups and exercises were performed. |

---

## Out of Scope (v1)

The following features are explicitly **not** included in version 1:

- Social features (friends, leaderboards, sharing)
- Nutrition tracking
- Wearable device integration (e.g., Apple Watch, Fitbit)
- Mobile app *(planned for a future version)*

---

## Success Criteria

| Feature | Definition of Success |
|---|---|
| **Onboarding + AI Plan** | A brand new user can sign up and receive their first AI-generated coaching plan in under 5 minutes. |
| **Daily Check-In** | The check-in form accurately captures the user's sleep, soreness, and energy level and feeds that data to the AI coach. |
| **Reminders** | Reminders are sent consistently and on schedule throughout the week. |
| **Progress Dashboard** | The dashboard reflects accurate past and present data, updating in real time as the user logs activity. |
| **Workout Logs** | Logs clearly illustrate which muscle groups were worked and which exercises targeted them, tracked on a daily basis. |

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

- LLM responses must return within **~10 seconds**.
- API keys and user data must **never be exposed** on the frontend.
- The app must function correctly on **desktop browsers**.
- Mobile is out of scope for v1 and is planned for a future release.

---

## User Stories

> Format: *"As a [persona], I want to [action], so that [benefit]."*

1. **AI Coaching Plan** — As a beginner who doesn't have time to plan my own workouts, I want an AI coach to give me optimal workouts, so that I can see results in muscle gain.

2. **Reminders** — As someone who works many hours a week, I want to receive reminders so I don't miss my scheduled workouts, so that I can keep a consistent schedule throughout the week.

3. **Workout Logs** — As a busy worker with minimal time, I want workout logs, so that I can keep track of the muscle groups I have worked on.

4. **Progress Dashboard** — As a college student who is used to tracking progress in small steps, I want a progress dashboard, so that I can keep track of my muscle-gaining goals.

5. **Daily Check-In** — As a worker who tends to push off tasks unrelated to the job, I want a daily check-in system that records my sleep, soreness, and energy level, so that the AI coach can adjust my plan accordingly.
